# <One line naming what the product should be able to do>

- **Status**: open | closed
- **Size**: small | medium | large (a session, a few sessions, a plan under `docs/plans/`)
- **Area**: <one of this repo's components>
- **Raised**: <yyyy-mm-dd>
- **Triaged**: <yyyy-mm-dd> now | next | someday | won't (omit until a triage pass has looked at it)

## What is missing

What a person or an agent cannot do today, and what they do instead.

## What it should do

The behaviour asked for, stated as what the product does once this lands. Say what is
explicitly not being asked for when the boundary is easy to get wrong.

## Why

What the request is worth, and to whom. A measurement, a size or a cost in time makes the
case concrete.

## Where it lands

The files or subsystems that would change, as `path/to/file.ext:42`. Write "Unknown" if
nobody has looked yet.

## How to do it

The steps, as far as they are known, and anything already measured that a later session
should not have to work out again. Write "Unknown" rather than guessing.

## What would make it wrong

The constraints that decide the shape: a platform limit, an invariant it must not break,
a cost that rules an approach out.

## Notes

Related issues and features as a link to their file, prior art, and anything ruled out.

## Outcome

Filled in when the request moves to `closed/`. What shipped, or why it will not be built:
a commit, a pull request, or a plan under `docs/plans/`.
